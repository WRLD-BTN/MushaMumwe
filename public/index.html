Install the SMCH App

Because you are on a local network (HTTP), your browser requires a manual step to add the app to your home screen or desktop.

  • On iPhone/Safari: Tap the Share icon (⎋) and select "Add to Home Screen".
  • On PC/Chrome: Click the 3 dots (⋮) and select "Install App".
